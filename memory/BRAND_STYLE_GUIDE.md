# Where The Crowlands — Full Brand Style Guide
## For Claude AI Reference: Content, Social Media, and Voice Alignment
### Last updated: March 2026

---

## 1. Core Identity

**Brand name:** Where The Crowlands
**Styled as:** nOcult
**Product type:** Web application for AI-generated DIY rituals guided by ancestral archetypes.
**Core premise:** Magic as a psychological and narrative tool for self-reflection, grounded in documented occult history and folklore. Not supernatural. Not therapeutic. Not a horoscope app.
**Primary symbol:** Crow / Raven (ancestral messengers).

### Mission
To offer a space for intentional self-reflection through the language and ritual structures of documented folk and occult traditions, guided by distinct ancestral voices.

### What We Are / What We Are Not
| We Are | We Are Not |
|--------|-----------|
| Historically grounded | Supernatural claims |
| Psychologically aware | Therapy or medical advice |
| Narratively rich | Vague "good vibes" content |
| Evidence-citing | Unsourced mysticism |
| Invitation-based | Predictive or prescriptive |

## 3. Visual Identity

### Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Primary background | Deep Teal | #0E2A2F | Main app backgrounds, hero sections |
| Secondary background | Celestial Blue | #123A3F | Layered surfaces, cards |
| Reading surface | Vellum / Bone Ivory | #F3EFE8 | All body text containers |
| Accent | Antique Gold | #C8A44D | Borders, linework, ornamental strokes ONLY |
| CTA | Ember Pink | #B94E6A | Call-to-action buttons only |
| Deep accent | Oxblood Burgundy | #8B2232 | Shigg's signature; secondary accents |
| Theresa threshold | Iron Grey | (context-specific) | Theresa's threshold workings, Cerberus imagery |

**Gold rule (non-negotiable):** Antique Gold (#C8A44D) is used for strokes and linework only. Never as a flat fill on any surface, background, or solid element. Violating this collapses the luminous quality of the aesthetic.

**Surface rule:** Body text must always sit on solid surfaces — Vellum (#F3EFE8) or Dark (#0E2A2F / #123A3F). Never on gradients or textured image overlays.

### Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| Display / Titles | TC Phantasmagoria | Hero headings, guide names |
| Display / Subheads | Cinzel Decorative | Section headers, ornamental text |
| Body (all readable prose) | Crimson Text (serif) | Always for running text. Never swap to sans-serif. |
| UI elements | Montserrat | Navigation, labels, buttons, metadata |

**Typography rules:**
- Never use sans-serif for body text.
- Minimum 14px for all body copy.
- Crimson Text must always be used for readable prose — non-negotiable.
- Display faces are for decoration and hierarchy only, not extended reading.

### Visual Aesthetic — Full Description

**Core aesthetic:** "Luminous Art Nouveau + Occult."

This means: ornate silk scarf tapestry illustration quality, engraved linework, Art Nouveau filigree borders, symmetrical medallion layouts, collector plate finish. The feeling is museum-quality, candlelit, archival — not digital, not modern, not pop.

**Illustration style:** All original illustration work uses fine engraved linework with controlled hatching. Think natural history illustration meets tarot deck meets Victorian botanical plate. Colour fills are muted, layered, and reference the core palette.

**Pattern and border usage:** Art Nouveau filigree borders frame content, particularly on cards, ritual pages, and featured guide content. Borders use Antique Gold strokes only — never fills.

**Symmetry:** Medallion and symmetrical layouts are preferred for featured content. Asymmetry is permissible in layout grids but not in decorative elements.

**Primary symbol usage:** The Crow/Raven appears as the central brand symbol — ancestral messenger, threshold creature. It should never be rendered in photorealistic, 3D, or cartoonish styles. Always engraved or illustrated in the established aesthetic.

**Cerberus visual rules (Theresa-specific):** Three-headed hound in engraved linework style (Art Nouveau, never realistic). Each head looking a different direction: Past (what was hidden), Present (what is being revealed), Future (what will come of this knowledge). Positioned at a threshold (doorway, gate, crossroads). Chain or collar with Hecate's key attached.

## 4. Brand Voice & Writing Rules

### Approved Terminology
The following terms are in active brand vocabulary:
- Working (not "spell" or "ritual" in promotional contexts)
- Practice
- Intention
- Guide (for the AI personas)
- "The tradition holds..."
- "The sources tell us..."
- Ancestral
- Lineage
- Threshold
- Liminality
- Sovereignty (Cathleen's protection workings)
- Crossing / Threshold investigation (Theresa's Hecate workings)
- Pattern recognition (preferred over "divination")
- Document / Documentation (the final offering)

### Forbidden Language
The following are banned across all brand communications:
- **"Manifest"** as a verb — wellness-app language that contradicts the brand's grounded stance.
- **"The Universe"** as an agent — implies supernatural claims the brand explicitly disavows.
- **"High vibes"** — wellness cliche with no meaning.
- **"Blessed be"** — overused neopagan signoff that feels generic.
- **"Love and light"** — signals the aesthetic we are explicitly not.
- **Vague certainty language** — ("You will find clarity", "This is your sign") — predictions, not invitations.
- **Medical or therapeutic claims** — ("This ritual will help with anxiety") — we are not therapists.
- **"So mote it be"** — generic neopagan.
- **"Toxic energy" / "Good vibes only"** — wellness-app vocabulary.
- **"Ancient wisdom"** (unspecified) — we cite specific traditions, not vague appeals to antiquity.
- **"Vibration" / "Frequency"** — new age vocabulary outside our register.

### Writing Guidelines

1. **Cite sources.** All historical or folkloric references must be attributable to a documented tradition, text, or cultural practice. Vague mysticism is not the brand.

2. **Use tendencies, not predictions.**
   - Correct: "The week may hold unexpected thresholds."
   - Wrong: "You will face a challenge this week."

3. **Grounded mysticism.** The magic is real in the sense that ritual, attention, and narrative have documented psychological effects. We can say that. We cannot claim supernatural outcomes.

4. **British English spelling throughout.** Colour, not color. Practise (verb), practice (noun). Analyse, not analyze.

5. **Minimum 14px body text.** Non-negotiable for accessibility and the brand's anti-digital aesthetic.

6. **No emoji in formal content.** Ritual pages, guide communications, lore content — emoji-free.

7. **Specificity over generality.** "A sprig of rosemary on the windowsill" is better than "some herbs." Specific images carry more weight.

8. **Opening with evocation.** All formal content should open with a specific sensory or narrative detail, not a thesis statement or hook.

9. **Pattern recognition, not divination.** When describing Theresa's Hecate workings or any investigative practice, frame it as "pattern recognition made ritual" — never as fortune-telling or psychic ability.

10. **Sovereignty language for Cathleen's protection work.** When the work is about land, home, or confronting external threats, the voice shifts from kitchen-practical to prophetic and implacable. This is Morrígan territory.

## 5. Content Strategy

### Content Pillars
1. **Lore Cards** — Single-subject deep dives into a documented folklore or occult tradition. Source always cited. Guide voice applied.
2. **Guide Wisdom** — Short-form guide-voiced reflections. Seasonal or thematic.
3. **Ritual Prompts** — Practical "working" invitations tied to a specific tradition or season.
4. **Historical Deep-Dives** — Longer-form pieces on specific traditions, figures, or texts. Katherine's domain primarily.
5. **Seasonal Content** — Calendar-based content tied to genuine historical and folkloric seasonal markers (not astrology-app versions).
6. **Threshold Investigations** — Theresa-voiced pieces on archival research, genealogy, and the ritual of uncovering hidden histories. May invoke Hecate/Cerberus framework.
7. **Sovereignty & Protection** — Cathleen-voiced pieces on holding ground, naming truth, and the Morrígan tradition of prophetic awareness.

### Social Media Strategy (Instagram)

**Aesthetic:** Every post maintains the midnight teal + antique gold + vellum palette. Ornate borders on every post.

**Tone:** Captions open with a specific, evocative detail. Reference real sources. Invite genuine engagement. No generic hooks ("Have you ever wondered..."). No hashtag spam.

**Content mix:**
- 25% Lore cards
- 20% Guide wisdom
- 20% Ritual prompts
- 15% Historical deep-dives
- 10% Threshold investigations / sovereignty content
- 10% Seasonal and timely content

**Caption structure:**
1. Specific evocative opening (1-2 sentences).
2. Historical or folkloric context (2-4 sentences, sourced).
3. Invitation or reflection (1-2 sentences).
4. Source attribution.
5. Minimal hashtags — never more than 5, always specific.

## 8. Mythological Integrations

### Cathleen x Morrígan
The Morrígan is the Irish goddess of war, fate, and sovereignty. In Cathleen's practice, she grants not visions but **attention** — the ability to see what is coming through pattern recognition older than anxiety. The crows are her messengers. Sovereignty work invokes her presence at land boundaries, thresholds, and moments of confrontation.

### Theresa x Hecate x Cerberus
Hecate is the Greek goddess of crossroads, keys, and torches. Cerberus is the three-headed hound who guards the threshold between worlds — and answers to Hecate. In Theresa's practice, Hecate grants passage and illumination; Cerberus provides protection during dangerous research. Together they form the complete threshold system: decision (Hecate) + enforcement (Cerberus). This is not divination. This is sanctified research.
