## 1. BRAND IDENTITY & POSITIONING

### Who We Are
**Where The Crowlands** (also styled as "nOcult") is a web application for AI-generated DIY rituals and spellwork guided by ancestral archetypes. We blend documented occult history, folklore, and myth with AI-powered personalization.

### Core Concept
Users interact with one of five ancestral "Guides" (AI personas) who help craft personalized workings based on intentions. Magic is treated as a psychological and narrative tool for self-reflection, not supernatural claims.

### Target Audience
- Modern witches, pagans, secular spiritualists
- Folklore enthusiasts and history lovers
- Ritual-as-meaning-making practitioners
- People drawn to Victorian/Edwardian occult aesthetics
- Those seeking self-reflection through ancestral connection
- Creatively inclined women 25-55 who value depth over trend

### Brand Personality
| Trait | Expression |
|-------|-----------|
| **Reverent** | We treat tradition with respect, not kitsch |
| **Accessible** | Warm and inviting, never gatekeeping |
| **Historically Grounded** | Every element has a real source or tradition behind it |
| **Warm but Not Saccharine** | Real emotion, not toxic positivity |
| **Intellectually Curious** | We cite sources, we research, we question |
| **Darkly Beautiful** | Aesthetic darkness as depth, never as edge |

### Brand Promise
*We offer a bridge between documented occult tradition and modern self-reflection, guided by ancestral voices who feel real because they're rooted in real history.*

### Tagline Options
- "The sacred lives in a cup of tea, a sprig of rosemary, a moment of silence."
- "Where ancestry meets intention."
- "Real history. Real ritual. Real you."

## 2. BRAND VOICE & LANGUAGE

### Tone Spectrum
```
Reverent --------[X]---- Casual
Warm ------------[X]---- Clinical
Poetic ----------[X]---- Plain
Grounded -------[X]----- Mystical
Inclusive ------[X]------ Exclusive
```

### Approved Language

**USE these terms:**
| Instead of... | Say... |
|---------------|--------|
| Spell | "Working" or "Practice" |
| Cast a spell | "Set an intention" or "Do the working" |
| Supernatural power | "Psychological tool" or "Narrative practice" |
| The Universe will... | "The tradition holds..." |
| Spirit guide | "Guide" or "Ancestral voice" |
| Magical | "Sacred" or "Meaningful" |
| Witchcraft | "Practice" or "The work" |
| New Age | (avoid entirely) |

**APPROVED vocabulary:**
- Working, Practice, Intention, Guide
- "The tradition holds..."
- "The sources tell us..."
- Ancestral, Lineage, Tradition, Archive
- Threshold, Liminality, Sacred ordinary
- Tendencies (not predictions)
- "Weather reports for the spirit"

**FORBIDDEN vocabulary:**
- "Manifest" (as a verb for wishing)
- "Universe" as an agent ("The Universe wants...")
- "High vibes" / "Raise your frequency"
- "Blessed be" / "So mote it be" (too cliche)
- "Align your vibration"
- "Manifest your destiny"
- Certainty language about outcomes
- Medical or therapeutic claims
- "Love and light"
- "Sacred feminine" (overused)

### Writing Style Rules
1. **Always cite sources** - Reference real books, real traditions, real history
2. **Tendencies, not predictions** - "The week may hold..." not "You will..."
3. **Grounded mysticism** - If it sounds like a horoscope app, rewrite it
4. **Specificity over vagueness** - Name the herb, the book, the year, the place
5. **Imperfection is welcome** - Chipped teacups, dog-eared books, ink-stained fingers
6. **British English** - Favour, colour, honour (the lineage is British)
7. **Seasonal awareness** - Reference actual solstices, equinoxes, and folk calendar

4. No pure black (`#000000`) anywhere - always use teal-blacks
5. Background gradients are atmospheric only - never under text

## 4. TYPOGRAPHY

### Font Stack (Priority Order)

| Role | Font | Weights | Usage |
|------|------|---------|-------|
| **Hero / Display** | TC Phantasmagoria | 400 | High-impact hero titles only. Sparingly used. |
| **Section Headers** | Cinzel Decorative | 400, 700, 900 | Page titles, form headers, section labels, CTA button text |
| **Subheadings** | Playfair Display | 400-800 | Secondary headers, card titles |
| **Body Text** | Crimson Text | 400, 600, 700 | All readable prose content, spell text, descriptions |
| **Elegant Body Alt** | EB Garamond | 400, 600 | Alternative body text, refined contexts |
| **Classic Heading Alt** | Cormorant Garamond | 400-700 | Alternative heading, editorial feel |
| **UI / Labels** | Montserrat | 300-600 | Buttons (when not Cinzel), metadata, navigation, small text |
| **Date / Accent** | Italiana | 400 | Dates, timeline labels, decorative text |
| **Fallback** | Georgia, serif | - | System fallback |

### Type Scale

```
Hero H1:     3rem / 5rem / 6rem (mobile / tablet / desktop)
             TC Phantasmagoria or Cinzel Decorative
             Letter-spacing: 0.15em
             Text-transform: uppercase

Section H2:  1.25rem / 1.5rem
             Cinzel Decorative or Playfair Display
             Letter-spacing: 0.1em

Card Title:  1.25rem
             Playfair Display
             Weight: 600-700

Body:        0.875rem / 1rem
             Crimson Text
             Line-height: 1.6-1.8
             Weight: 400 (regular), 600 (emphasis)

Labels:      0.75rem / 0.875rem
             Montserrat
             Text-transform: uppercase
             Letter-spacing: 0.15em
             Weight: 500-600

Dates:       0.875rem
             Italiana
             Letter-spacing: 0.05em
```

### Typography Rules
1. **Never use sans-serif for body text** - Always serif (Crimson Text primary)
2. **Phantasmagoria is precious** - Only for hero moments, never for navigation or labels
3. **Cinzel for authority** - Section titles, buttons, anything that needs to feel ceremonial
4. **Crimson for reading** - Anything longer than a label gets Crimson Text
5. **Montserrat for utility** - Small UI text, metadata, navigation items
6. **Minimum body size: 14px** - Readability is sacred

## 6. VISUAL DNA & ART DIRECTION

### Core Aesthetic: "Luminous Art Nouveau + Occult"

The visual language draws from four intersecting traditions:

1. **Art Nouveau Elegance** - Organic curves, halo arcs, botanical flows, Mucha-influenced linework
2. **Celestial Symbolism** - Lunar phases, sun discs, stars, planetary alignments
3. **Occult Iconography** - Ravens, all-seeing eyes, alchemical motifs, ceremonial tools
4. **Illuminated Manuscript Sensibility** - Vellum surfaces, gold linework, bordered pages

### The "Collectible Scarf" Aesthetic
All generated imagery should feel like a **luxurious silk scarf or tapestry illustration**:
- Ornate occult silk scarf illustration
- Ultra-detailed engraved linework
- Etched texture with art nouveau filigree border
- Symmetrical medallion layout
- Collector plate finish
- Velvet silk sheen with faint parchment undertone
- Antique print finish

### Art Direction Keywords (for AI image generation)
```
ornate occult silk scarf tapestry illustration, ultra-detailed engraved linework,
etched texture, art nouveau filigree border, symmetrical medallion layout,
collector plate finish, velvet silk sheen, midnight navy and oxblood and
antique gold and bone ivory palette, British folklore motifs
```

### Composition Rules
1. **Central medallion focus** - Symmetrical arrangement with a focal center
2. **Symmetrical border frames** - Balanced on all sides
3. **Corner flourishes** - Decorative accents at edges (structural, not decorative)
4. **Interstitial decorative bands** - Dividers between content sections
5. **Layered depth** - Atmospheric background, then solid reading surface, then content

### Key Visual Principles
1. **Ornament is structural** - It defines edges, sections, hierarchy. It's architecture, not decoration.
2. **Gold is stroke-only** - Linework, borders, glyphs. Never flat fills.
3. **Luminous, not distressed** - Clean, light paper grain. NOT heavy parchment or grunge texture.
4. **Readability is sacred** - WCAG AA contrast minimum. Content always on solid surfaces.
5. **BOLD expression** - Ornament strokes are 2-4px. Visible, confident, architectural.
6. **One atmospheric image per page** - Never let imagery compete with content.

## 10. COMPOSITION RULES & LAYOUT

### The Golden Rules of Layout

1. **Reading surfaces are sacred** - Any text longer than 2-3 lines MUST sit on a solid surface (Vellum or Dark Plate). No gradients, no overlays, no opacity tricks under text.

2. **Visual stacking order (top to bottom):**
   ```
   1. Text content
   2. Solid reading surface (Vellum or Dark)
   3. Ornament strokes (edges only)
   4. Texture/grain
   5. Background color
   ```

3. **Spacing is generous** - Use 2-3x more whitespace than feels comfortable. Let content breathe.

4. **Ornaments define structure** - Corner flourishes, dividers, and borders are architectural elements that create hierarchy. They're not decoration.

5. **Maximum one atmospheric image per page** - Never let imagery compete with content.

6. **Symmetry as default** - The Art Nouveau tradition favors balanced, symmetrical compositions.

### Corner Ornament Usage

| Context | Corner Type | Size |
|---------|------------|------|
| Homepage hero, major thresholds | Elaborate halo corners | 80px |
| Section headers, form panels | Standard halo corners | 45px |
| Cards, secondary elements | Simple corners | 30px |

### Divider Usage

| Context | Divider Type |
|---------|-------------|
| Major section breaks | Lunar phase divider |
| Sub-section breaks | Simple moon divider |
| Minimal separation | Simple line divider |

### Spacing Scale
```
Container padding:    2rem / 3rem / 5rem (mobile / tablet / desktop)
Section gaps:         2rem / 3rem
Card internal:        1.5rem
Element spacing:      1rem
Tight spacing:        0.5rem
```

### Contrast Requirements (WCAG AA)
- Body text: 4.5:1 minimum contrast ratio
- Headings: 3:1 minimum contrast ratio
- Gold on dark: Test carefully - increase font weight if needed

## 12. COPY & CONTENT GUIDELINES

### Headlines & Titles
- Use Cinzel Decorative energy: authoritative, ceremonial
- Keep under 8 words when possible
- Reference specific traditions, not vague mysticism
- **Good:** "The Rubáiyát Tea Ritual" / "What the Crows Carry" / "Parliament of Birds Oracle"
- **Bad:** "Unlock Your Inner Magic" / "Manifest Your Best Life" / "Spiritual Awakening Guide"

### Body Copy
- Crimson Text energy: warm, readable, grounded
- Lead with specific detail, not abstract concept
- Always include a source reference when citing tradition
- Break into short paragraphs (3-4 sentences max)
- Use active voice
- British English

### Call-to-Action Text
- Cinzel Decorative, uppercase, tracked wide
- Ritualistic but clear: "Begin the Working" / "Enter the Archive" / "Choose Your Guide"
- **Never:** "Click Here" / "Learn More" / "Sign Up Now" / "Get Started"

### Content Categories for Marketing

| Category | Description | Guide Affinity |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Folklore Facts** | Real historical traditions, referenced and sourced | All |
| **Practical Rituals** | Small, doable practices anyone can try | Shigg, Brenda |
| **Shadow Prompts** | Journaling and self-reflection questions | Katherine, Theresa |
| **Protection Work** | Boundary-setting practices, ward creation | Cathleen |
| **Seasonal Calendar** | Solstices, equinoxes, folk dates, moon phases | All |
| **Source Spotlights** | Deep dives into referenced authors and works | Katherine, Theresa |
| **Memory & Grief** | Honoring loss, ancestral connection | Shigg, Brenda |
| **Bird Oracle** | Messages and meanings from bird sightings | Shigg |
| **Family Patterns** | Inherited behaviors, breaking cycles | Theresa |

### Email / Newsletter Voice
- Subject lines: Specific and evocative, never clickbait
  - **Good:** "Three crows on the fence before seven" / "The marigold threshold practice"
  - **Bad:** "You won't BELIEVE what the cards say!" / "5 spells to change your life"
- Opening: Always a scene or detail, never "Hi [Name]!"
- Tone: Like a letter from a thoughtful friend who happens to know a lot about folklore
- Sign-off: "From the Crowlands" or guide-specific
